# XEDO 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

> **Product:** XEDO  
> **Tagline:** Detect. Connect. Predict. Protect.  
> **Core Position:** "XEDO doesn't just detect digital threats. It connects the evidence, discovers potential threat campaigns, explains the risk, predicts emerging patterns, and guides users from suspicion to lawful action."

---

## 🕒 The 3-Minute Presentation Walkthrough

### 0:00 – 0:30 | The Problem & Landing Page
1. **Open Landing Page** (`http://localhost:5174/#landing`).
2. Point out the clean Apple-level aesthetic: light theme default, high whitespace, calm and trustworthy.
3. Hover over the interactive **Centerpiece Hero Graph**:
   - Hover over `Social Profile` -> Show "Potential Impersonation (94% mimicry)".
   - Hover over `Website` -> Show "Phishing Landing Page".
4. State the core message: *"Today's cyber threats are no longer isolated scams; they are coordinated campaigns. XEDO takes citizens and analysts from initial suspicion to lawful action."*

### 0:30 – 1:00 | Universal Threat Scan & Explainable AI
1. Click **"Analyze a Threat"** or select the top demo preset: `@sbi_supportt`.
2. Click **"Analyze with XEDO AI"**.
3. Watch the multi-step investigation sequence:
   - *Collecting signals → Comparing identities → Checking similarity → Evaluating risk → Building threat graph → Generating explanation.*
4. Show the animated **Risk Engine Gauge**: **91 / 100 HIGH RISK**.
5. Highlight the **"Why XEDO thinks this is risky"** explainability card:
   - Notice the ethical non-criminal phrasing: *"This profile closely resembles verified patterns of State Bank of India while operating from unauthorized external channels."*
   - Show the 5-part breakdown bars: Brand Similarity 94%, Username Similarity 89%, URL Similarity 96%, Content Risk 82%, Identity Signals 91%.

### 1:00 – 1:40 | Threat Graph & Autonomous AI Investigation
1. Click **"View Threat Graph"** (`#threat-graph`).
2. Show how XEDO automatically connects:
   - **Official Brand** → **Lookalike Profile** (`@sbi_supportt`) → **Phishing URL** (`sbi-kyc-verification.top`) → **Scam SMS** → **Trojanized APK** (`SBI_Secure_v4.2.apk`).
3. Click the node **"@sbi_supportt"** to reveal the slide-over Threat Intelligence drawer.
4. Click the prominent button: **"Investigate with XEDO AI"**.
5. Watch the animated investigation modal tally live:
   - **17 signals analyzed**
   - **6 related entities linked**
   - **3 high-risk indicators**
   - **1 coordinated campaign**
   - **8 evidence items cryptographically sealed**

### 1:40 – 2:15 | Differentiators: Identity DNA & Threat Forecast
1. Switch to **Digital Identity DNA™** (`#threat-detail` or `#identity-dna`).
   - Show the biometric-style fingerprint comparison: *Verified Sovereign Baseline vs Analyzed External Footprint*.
   - Point out the 93% Deception Risk index.
2. Open **AI Threat Forecast** (`#forecast`).
   - Highlight **EMERGING PATTERN DETECTED** banner:
   - Current risk: High | Predicted trend: Increasing | Potential next vector: *Fake customer support → automated WhatsApp bots → malicious APK downloads*.
   - Show the Past → Current → Projected trajectory timeline.

### 2:15 – 2:40 | From Suspicion to Lawful Action (NCRP & 1930)
1. Switch to **Safety Navigator** (`#safety-center`):
   - Click *"YES — Money was debited"* to demonstrate the emergency protocol.
   - Show Helpline **1930** emergency alert.
2. Click **"Prepare Complaint"** (`#report`):
   - Click **"Generate Complaint Summary"**.
   - Show the formatted narrative with SHA-256 evidence digests.
   - Point out the **"Continue to Official NCRP"** button leading directly to `https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/`.
   - Point out the clear legal disclaimer: *"XEDO is an independent platform and not a government website."*

### 2:40 – 3:00 | Security Analyst Console & Copilot
1. Click the top DemoBar to toggle role to **Security Analyst / Admin** (`#command-center`).
2. Show the **SOC Metrics**, **Live Threat Queue**, and **Simulated Regional Threat Map** (Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi).
3. Open **XEDO Copilot** (`#copilot`):
   - Click the prompt chip: *"Summarize this investigation."*
   - Watch the copilot immediately synthesize Case XD-1024 without fabricating facts.
4. Conclude:
   *"XEDO bridges the gap from suspicion to detection, evidence, correlation, risk understanding, and official reporting. Detect. Connect. Predict. Protect."*
